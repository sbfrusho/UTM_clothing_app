# Your Shop: Privacy policy

_Last updated 18 November 2026_

The in-app copy is in `lib/modules/legal/legal_content.dart`. Keep the two in sync. Replace `[support email]` before you publish.

## Who we are
Your Shop is an online clothing shop. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It follows the laws of Bangladesh.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your name, email address, phone number and, if you add one, a profile photo.
- **Delivery:** the addresses you save.
- **Shopping:** your cart, saved items and orders.
- **Device:** a notification token so we can send order updates, and basic diagnostics when something goes wrong.

Card details are entered in Stripe's secure payment sheet and never reach our servers or the app.

## How we use it
To create your account, deliver and support your orders, take payments, send updates about your orders, and keep the shop secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Who processes it
Data is stored with Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, notifications). Card payments are processed by Stripe. Both act on our behalf under their own privacy commitments.

## How long we keep it
Your account data stays until you delete your account. Orders are kept for accounting and tax purposes. Once the account is deleted, they no longer include your name, email, phone or address.

## Your choices
- Edit your name, phone, photo and addresses in **Profile**.
- Turn order notifications off in your phone settings.
- Delete your account and its data at any time in **Profile → Delete account**.

To ask for a copy of your data, or with any other question, contact us at [support email].

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
When this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
